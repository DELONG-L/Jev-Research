# Covers and visual sources / 封面与素材来源

[English home](../README.md) · [中文首页](../README.zh-CN.md)

The covers follow the explorer’s midnight navy, ice-blue signals, and restrained amber accents. A conceptual loop connects intent and network state, decision, execution, and verification. It illustrates the research questions shared by the three studies; it does not depict measured performance or a particular network deployment.

封面沿用研究页面的午夜蓝、冰蓝连线和少量琥珀色点缀，以概念控制环连接意图与网络状态、决策、执行和验证。示意图表达三项研究共同关注的问题，不表示实测性能或特定网络部署。

The large English title uses a regular book serif; the Chinese title pairs Roman letters with upright Songti. Subtitles and diagram labels keep their sans-serif styling. Chinese characters retain natural square proportions. No experimental curves, numerical results, manuscript text, identities, or external photography were used to generate the covers.

英文大标题使用常规书籍衬线体，中文大标题使用直立宋体；副标题与示意图标签保持无衬线字形。中文保持自然、方正的字面比例。生成素材未使用实验曲线、数值结果、稿件正文、个人身份或外部摄影作品。
